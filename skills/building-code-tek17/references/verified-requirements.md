## Rooms

**Source:** [TEK17 §12-7](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/12/ii/12-7),
paragraphs 1–3, associated guidance and FAQ; retrieved 2026-09-11.

- **Regulation, (1):** shape, height and size must suit the room's function.
- **Regulation, (2)(a)/(b):** ordinary dwelling rooms for permanent occupancy have
  a 2.4 m minimum height; other rooms have 2.2 m. Paragraph (2)(c)/(d) contains
  special categories and partial-height provisions; do not turn (a) into a rule
  for all rooms or interventions.
- **Regulation, (3) and guidance:** use change between supplementary/main parts
  of a dwelling has its own height route. Guidance describes 2.20 m and a 2.00 m
  route for dwellings whose erection was applied for before 1 July 2011. Verify
  every condition and measurement convention; building age alone is insufficient.
- **FAQ:** no fixed minimum room area. The former 7 m² recommendation was not a
  statutory minimum. Function, furniture, access, ventilation and other applicable
  requirements still need assessment.

**Correction:** the former universal 7 m²/2.20 m claim is withdrawn. Do not use it
to reject or approve a room, including in downstream tender or drawing reviews.

## Energy

**Sources:** [§14-2](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/14/14-2)
and [§14-3](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/14/14-3),
regulation and guidance; retrieved 2026-09-11.

§14-2 first paragraph combines the applicable energy frame with §14-3. The dwelling
energy-measures route and permitted redistribution have separate conditions.
Do not substitute §14-3 minimum levels for the complete applicable pathway.

For the category stated in **§14-3(1)(a)** (excluding the specified log-wall
dwellings/holiday homes), the inspected regulation lists:

| Property | Minimum-level limit | Units |
|---|---|---|
| External wall U-value | ≤ 0.22 | W/(m²K) |
| Roof U-value | ≤ 0.18 | W/(m²K) |
| Floor to ground/outside U-value | ≤ 0.18 | W/(m²K) |
| Window/door including frame U-value | ≤ 1.2 | W/(m²K) |
| Leakage at 50 Pa | ≤ 1.5 | h⁻¹ |

**Applicability hold:** determine actual §14-1/§14-5 and existing-building scope,
selected energy pathway, relevant averages and calculation basis. These are not
default product targets, a complete energy design or acceptance of an assembly.
The former skill table mixed measures/minima and is withdrawn.

## Escape Windows

**Source:** [§11-13](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/11/iv/11-13),
third paragraph and **preaccepted guidance**, including figure 5; retrieved
2026-09-11. These figures are guidance for that route, not unconditional statutory
dimensions for every window or every room.

- Clear opening height at least 0.6 m, width at least 0.5 m, sum at least 1.5 m;
  pivot windows need an equivalent effective opening.
- Floor-to-bottom-of-opening distance at most 1.0 m unless measures facilitate
  escape; readily operable without special tools and hinged for easy exit.
- The inspected risk-class-4 guidance refers to at least every second room for
  permanent occupancy, not every bedroom as a blanket rule. Distribution, storey
  exits, terrain height, rescue access and the full fire strategy still matter.

**Window-well hold:** the source does not supply a universal lysgrav plan/depth
dimension. Establish usable geometry with the actual sash, finished surfaces,
steps/cover, access, terrain and water/ground design. Nominal frame size is not
free opening. A dimensional arithmetic check alone never approves escape.

## Moisture

**Source:** [§13-13](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/13/vi/13-13),
regulation and preaccepted guidance; retrieved 2026-09-11.

The regulation requires design/execution avoiding moisture damage from condensed
indoor vapour. The inspected preaccepted guidance describes an air/vapour-tight
layer normally on the warm side of insulation, an outward vapour-open wind barrier
facing a ventilated cavity, airtight joints and drying capability. It states an
Sd value of at least 10 m for the vapour barrier in that guidance route.

This is not a universal membrane specification for every retrofit, below-ground
wall or high-humidity use. An assembly-specific assessment and exact product data
remain necessary. No replacement for the former contradictory cold/warm ratio is
asserted. Paid SINTEF details and material-standard tables were not accessed.

## Stormwater

**Source:** [§15-8](https://www.dibk.no/regelverk/byggteknisk-forskrift-tek17/15/iii/15-8),
first paragraph and guidance; retrieved 2026-09-11.

The inspected paragraph sizes infiltration, detention and conveyance together
for climate-adjusted 100-year rainfall **unless otherwise determined in the land-use
plan**. Guidance describes measure scope and an earlier-application transition.
This replaces the former unsupported generic 10-year design event; it does not
make 100-year rainfall an unconditional rule for every existing drain.

Check actual municipal plan, application/measure scope, local rainfall data,
catchment, infiltration and receiving/overflow capacity. A climate factor, pipe
diameter, fall or discharge connection cannot be invented from this paragraph.

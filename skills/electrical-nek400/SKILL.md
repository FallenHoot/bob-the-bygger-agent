# Skill: Electrical Coordination — Norway

## Purpose

Help identify electrical interfaces, required evidence and questions for the
appropriate qualified electrical enterprise/designer. Explain concepts without
presenting an installation design, permission to work or commissioning approval.

## Trust Boundary

BTBA may explain source-backed requirements, organize equipment/load records and
flag missing evidence. Any sizing arithmetic is preliminary and explicitly scoped;
actual circuit/protection/earthing design requires a competent designer's assessment
of the installation and applicable Norwegian requirements. Do not instruct users
to work on exposed live parts or bypass protection. Immediate danger takes priority
under the system prompt; isolate only if safe and contact appropriate assistance.

Check task-specific registration, competence and permitted-work boundaries through
current DSB/FEL/FEK sources. Do not substitute a generic foreign licence, “strømmeister”
label or presumed registration for evidence. DSB is the national electrical safety
authority; municipal building permission does not establish electrical safety.

The former universal cable/breaker/RCD tables, system prevalence, electrode targets,
NIOSH voltage-drop citation, standard titles and automatic insurance-voiding claims
are withdrawn. This correction does not certify a replacement electrical rule pack.

## Inputs

- Actual work: explanation, interface coordination, modification or assessment.
- Supply system and voltage/phases from reliable records or competent inspection;
  do not assume TT/TN/IT from geography, building age or a photograph.
- Existing distribution, protection, earthing/bonding and condition evidence.
- Exact equipment nameplate/manufacturer requirements; distinguish heat output,
  electrical input, starting/inrush current and continuous duty.
- Cable route, length, installation method, grouping, ambient conditions and other
  relevant design factors; missing inputs prevent a project sizing recommendation.
- Actual responsible electrical enterprise/designer and accepted scope, or unassigned.
- Applicable standard edition, FEL/FEK provisions and documentation requirements.

## Workflow

1. Scope the task and review relevant evidence only. A terminology question does
   not require an address, site scan or complete electrical design.
2. Establish the legal/standard/product basis. Distinguish regulations from standards
   used to document safety and from project preferences. Do not assert that every
   departure from NEK 400 is automatically illegal or voids insurance.
3. Inventory proposed loads and interfaces with actual units and source status.
   A nameplate total alone is not a demand/diversity or protective-device design.
4. Identify affected circuits, distribution capacity, earthing/bonding, protective
   measures, isolation, service access and building/fire-envelope penetrations.
5. Refer actual sizing/protection/disconnection/short-circuit verification and
   special-location requirements to the responsible electrical designer. Do not
   select breaker curves or RCD types from generic appliance categories.
6. Return source-linked observations, unassessed items, coordination questions and
   the next evidence/reviewer required. Separate proposed work from installed/tested
   conditions and actual samsvarserklæring or other documentation.
7. Preserve revisions and release holds through [project-lifecycle](../project-lifecycle/SKILL.md)
   when changes affect details, quantities, design or construction sequencing.

## Cross-Trade Interfaces

| Scope | Coordination questions, not installation instructions |
|---|---|
| Wall/opening changes | Which cables/outlets cross the affected area? Who verifies isolation/relocation before disturbance? How are air/fire seals and access preserved? |
| Heat pump | Actual electrical input and manufacturer supply/protection requirements, isolator/access, condensate and mechanical interfaces |
| EV charging | Actual supply/capacity/load management, earthing/protection basis, route, environment and commissioned equipment |
| Solar/storage | AC/DC isolation and protection, equipment compatibility, structural/fire/roof penetrations, grid connection and responsible scopes |
| Wet/outdoor areas | Actual environmental/location classification and product/protection requirements; no generic wet-room breaker size |
| Temporary works | Safe temporary supply, protection, access and documented responsibility; no improvised live-work or extension-cord design |

## Sources and Verification Status

Start with [DSB electrical safety](https://www.dsb.no/elsikkerhet/),
[DSB electrical installations and equipment](https://www.dsb.no/elsikkerhet/elektriske-anlegg-og-utstyr/),
[Lovdata](https://lovdata.no/) and [NEK](https://www.nek.no/).
Record exact document/paragraph/edition and retrieval date before citing a rule.
Authorized standard text may be required. Do not fabricate clauses when access fails.

On 2026-09-11 the DSB landing page identified its national authority role; specific
FEL/FEK/NEK provisions and work exemptions were not independently validated here.
The links are retrieval starting points, not a claim that all requirements were checked.
Insurance and contractual consequences need the actual policy/contract and qualified
review; do not infer them from a technical deviation or missing document.

## Output and Synthetic Cases

Output a concise coordination record: affected item/location, source/revision,
observed/reported/proposed state, missing evidence, responsible reviewer and next action.
Generated technical documents carry the AI advisory draft label; no “safe to energize”
or completed inspection claim without actual qualified evidence for that scope.

- Fictional heat pump advertises a thermal capacity only: electrical demand remains
  unknown; request electrical nameplate/product data, do not size a circuit.
- Fictional wall removal has undocumented cable routes: identify isolation and
  inspection needs; do not declare the wall clear or prescribe live testing.
- Missing declaration: record an evidence gap and seek the actual record; not proof
  that the installation is illegal, insured/uninsured or safe.
